Monster Mines – a twist on a classic

A game created for XBOX gamejam with the base idea of a classic mine sweeper developed into a horror game.

Overview

Monster Mines is a game-jam protitype created for XBOX gamejam that was part of a collaboration between XBOX and Howest University. The game is a horror twist on the classic minesweeper game, where players must navigate a minefield while avoiding monsters that are attracted to their movements. Test the mines and see if they are just a mine or a monster waiting for you.

My role

I worked as idea generator and game and level designer, responsible for the main gameplay loop and game mechanics. I also worked on the visual design and feedback to enhance the overall experience.

  • Defined the core loop based on minesweeper mechanics.
  • Designed and created the level in Unity.
  • Helped with perfecting the visual feedback and programming part.

Design goals

  • Keep the game feel close to minesweeper.
  • Make players think before they do an action just like in the classic.
  • If player judges wrong he should still have a chance to recover.

Process & iteration

The project was created in 2.5 days which included the ideation, prototyping and finalization of the game. The first iteration was a simple prototype with basic mechanics test. After testing the game we realized that the game was too hard and players were not able to recover from their mistakes. We completely scraped one of the mechancis and changed it to another one which in perspective was a good move.

What I learned

  • Horror games require a lot of polishing and balancing.
  • Changing one game into another is a unique and interesting way to create something new.
  • Polishing phase needs way more time than you usually expect.
