Project

Monster Mines

A horror game inspired by Minesweeper about clearing a monster‑infested bunker, built during a game jam.

Role & team

Game designer & programmer · Unity URP · Team jam project.

Design goals

  • Capture Minesweeper’s tension in a first‑person horror context.
  • Make risk–reward decisions readable and fast to understand.
  • Keep runs short but intense to encourage “one more attempt”.

What I did

  • Designed the core loop and bunker layout logic.
  • Tuned pacing and monster encounters to keep tension constant.
  • Implemented core mechanics and basic feedback in Unity.

What I learned

How small changes in information clarity and audio/visual cues drastically change perceived fairness and fear, and how to keep a jam scope under control while still delivering a complete loop.
