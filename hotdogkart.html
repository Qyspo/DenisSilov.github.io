Project · Hot Dog Kart

Hot Dog Kart – a tasty and fast transportation

A kart made for a racing game for course of 3d modeling made with an idea to make something fun and unique.

Overview

HotDog Kart is a model that was created for an arcade-racing game as part of a 3D modeling course. The task was to create a unique and fun vehicle that would stand out in the game rather than a standart kart. The design of the kart was inspired by the idea of a hot dog, with a playful and whimsical aesthetic.

My role

I designed, modeled, textured and animated the kart, focusing on a strong silhouette, clear materials, and a fun overall shape that would fit an arcade-style racing game.

  • Developed and designed the core idea of the kart.
  • Fully modeled the kart in Blender and textured in Substance Painter.
  • Perfected presentation with rigging and animation.

Design goals

  • Create a vehicle that feels humorous and instantly recognizable.
  • Keep the design readable from a distance with a strong silhouette.
  • Make the model feel suitable for a fun, arcade-style racing game.

Process & iteration

This kart was created in a few weeks and went through several iterations. The initial idea was a simple hot dog shape with some wheels attached to it and a simple seat, but it lacked the fun and playful aesthetic that I was aiming for. I then experimented with different shapes and materials, eventually settling on a more sillly and whimsical design that emphasized the kart with more realistic engine plus seat and hotdog with pickle wheels and sauce pipes that go into sauce bottles - exhaust .

What I learned

  • Combining realism with whimsy can create a unique and memorable design.
  • The simple design choices in the beggining require more time to make them interesting in the end.
  • Focusing on small details doesnt always matter, and sometimes u need to look at bigger picture.
