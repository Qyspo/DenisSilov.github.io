About

Who is causing all this?

I’m an extroverted goblin who likes cycling, slightly unhinged ideas, and pressing “build” at 3 AM. I’d rather ship a weird prototype than keep a “perfect” idea trapped in a Google Doc.

Most days I live in Unity (HDRP/URP) and C#, bouncing between design and implementation so I can break things fast and fix them slightly slower. I love core loops, systems that talk to each other, and giving players decisions that feel risky but fair.
